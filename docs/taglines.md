# Unconsumed Games: tagline options

The chosen tagline is **Games that abide.** It comes from 1 Corinthians 3:14: "If any man's work abide which he hath built thereupon, he shall receive a reward." Scripture is in King James wording.

Where the other lines are used on the site:

- "Burning, yet not consumed." is the home-page motto section.
- "Turn aside and see." is the hero call to action.
- "Not burned up. Made to last." is a home-page heading.
- "Light after darkness" is the gloss on "Post tenebras lux".

| Tagline                         | Meaning or source                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Games that abide.               | **Chosen.** 1 Cor 3:14: work that survives the fire.                                                          |
| Burning, yet not consumed.      | The English sense of Exodus 3:2: "the bush burned with fire, and the bush was not consumed."                  |
| Work that abides.               | 1 Cor 3:14.                                                                                                   |
| Turn aside and see.             | Exodus 3:3: "I will now turn aside, and see this great sight."                                                |
| Untouched by the fire.          | H.T.'s line. Daniel 3:27: the fire had no power on them.                                                      |
| Through the fire, not burned.   | Isaiah 43:2: "when thou walkest through the fire, thou shalt not be burned."                                  |
| Tried by fire. Made to last.    | 1 Cor 3:13: "the fire shall try every man's work of what sort it is."                                         |
| Gold, silver, precious stones.  | 1 Cor 3:12: the materials that survive the fire, as opposed to wood, hay and stubble.                         |
| No smell of fire.               | Daniel 3:27: "nor the smell of fire had passed on them."                                                      |
| Not burned up. Made to last.    | The brand's own phrase.                                                                                       |
| Kindled, never consumed.        | The bush lit by God's presence (Exodus 3).                                                                    |
| Post tenebras lux.              | "After darkness, light": the Reformation motto.                                                               |
| Light after darkness.           | The English form of Post tenebras lux.                                                                        |
| Stories that stand for ever.    | Isaiah 40:8: "the word of our God shall stand for ever."                                                      |
| Played today. Kept for life.    | From the site's Lasting copy.                                                                                 |
| Faithful stories, made to last. | The older tagline ("Faithful stories, well made.") combined with the brand phrase.                            |
| Thy years shall have no end.    | Psalm 102:27. It speaks of God, so use it as a verse on a page rather than as a studio claim.                 |
| Holy ground, well made.         | Exodus 3:5: "the place whereon thou standest is holy ground." Use with care so it doesn't sound like a boast. |
| Through the fire, unconsumed.   | Combines Isaiah 43:2 with the studio name.                                                                    |

_Nec tamen consumebatur_ ("yet it was not consumed") is the Church of Scotland's motto. It is listed for reference only; we advise against using it as the studio's tagline or on merch.
